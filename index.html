Frequently Asked Q’s

What is SpaceUp? What services do you offer? Why should I choose SpaceUp?

SpaceUp is a full-service professional interior design firm, led by dynamic architects and interior designers focused on making designer homes personalised to your tastes and needs available within your budget. We aim to bring premium design and quality to each and every home buyer.

Our services include interior designing, custom furniture, false ceiling, flooring, wall accents, and so much more.

We deliver high-quality interiors because we have a 100% in-house team, whose work we strictly monitor based on the quality checklist. And that’s why we can even give you a full home warranty!

Do you charge a design fee?

No, Design services are free of cost for Rooms that are part of the order.

When does that custom design process start?

The custom design process starts after you sign up with Spaceup and have paid the 10% advance.

How long does the design process take?

Since each SpaceUp home is designed and custom made for you, it takes between 10 -15 days to design based on your requirements and feedback. Your designer will give you a more accurate timeline at your first meeting.

What are the safety measures taken to do my home interiors?

We sure understand your concern. This is why we have implemented a strict no-contact policy. With temperature checks, fumigation, online meetings, and all other necessary safety protocols maintained at every interaction point, we’ve got your safety well covered at our Experience centre and on- site works . So let go of your worries and kick start your dream today.

How is the pricing calculated?

Each home has its own needs and requirements based on its size, its design, material finish and hardware used as well storage options. The prices are generated dynamically from our software based on your custom design, giving you full flexibility to create designer homes

How is SpaceUp different from contractors/interior design firms/studios?

It’s almost like night and day. On one hand, you have extensive self-research, numerous market trips trying to choose materials, finishes and furniture, and the endless confusion of coordinating work on site. On the other hand, you can have the bright experience we offer with its cutting-edge technology. We provide you everything you’ll ever need under one — designers who are able to understand your needs and transform them to designs, expert modular installation partners, reliable warranties and much more.

Can I get just a part of my home designed?

Your dream is our dream. Small or big, we’re equally excited - even if that means just getting a modular kitchen. However, to make it worth the time and effort, we’ve set a minimum budget of ₹2 lakhs. The simplest way to find out if your scope of work fits, is to have a quick conversation with one of our designers.

Do you offer any warranty?

Yes, you get a full home warranty! This means when you design your interiors with us, you not only get up to a 10-years warranty on our catalogue products but also a 1-year warranty on our services

I want to do my interiors. But I don't have a huge budget,can SpaceUp help me with the budget

Spaceup designers work within the budget specified by you. They will design and suggest options that work within the budget you have in mind without compromising style or functionality.

How can Spaceup help me with budget planning

We have got you covered, you can design now and pay in instalments pay later. We’ve partnered with leading financial institutions for easy monthly instalments. Contact your designer to know more.
