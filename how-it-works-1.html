How It Works

  • Home
  • /
  • How It Works

1

The first step in the process is interacting with our designer who will be able to get a fair idea of your vision for your house. At this stage, the designer will be able to give you an initial personalised design and a ballpark quotation for your dream home.

2

At this stage, once you're happy with what we have proposed, get us on-board by paying 5% of the quotation. We now dive deep into the details. Choose your favorite materials and finishes from a wide array of choices.

3

For a cohesive treatment to your interiors, we ensure to take accurate measurements that help us in creating perfect designs.

4

Once the designs are liked and approved by you, we get the ball rolling on the sourcing and procurement of your dream home. At this stage, we will be able to share an accurate quotation for all services.

5

At this stage, you will need to process 50% of the payment. We will kick start the civil work at the site.

6

You can stay updated with the project status by tracking the updates on the SpaceUp mobile app.

7

At this stage, we are halfway through your project, the manufacturing of orders is in process. The orders get delivered on-site and the installation is scheduled as per the design.

8

Your house is now your home! It's time to make memories!
