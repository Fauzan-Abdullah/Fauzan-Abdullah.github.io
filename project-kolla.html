PROJECT DETAILS

Area 5775 sqft
Location Kondapur, Hyderabad
Type Villa

The residence is designed for a family of four, the couple and for their two young daughters. The villa located in Kondapur area of Hyderabad comprises three levels; the ground floor with the Drawing room, Kitchen, Living and Dining; the first floor consists of a prayer room, family lounge, bedrooms and Study while the third floor consists of bedroom, home theatre and terrace.

The brief from the client was to have a family home designed for the extended family and parents to come over for visits. The house had to be a reflection of the personality of the couple and also looking forwards to the aspirations of the couple.

The drawing and living rooms room are treated with exquisite Italian marble dado and also has a flavour of the personalities of the couple. The Moroccan themed wallpaper with a neutralizing feature of wood, all come together to form a coherent texture for the space.

The bedrooms are treated with care and soft fabric with plush cushioned headboards. The daughters' rooms are treated with features, which will provide them with the interest and inquisitiveness coming off the wall elements. The subtle pastel colors provide space in the rooms and avoids over-engaging visually.

The first floor family lounge is a representation of the upward movement and the plush seating with the flamboyant marble make this complete.
